DEMONSTRATION PROCEDURE — fictional, not an Aeiforia policy or legal guidance.

SECTION 1: Partial payments
When the received payment is lower than the invoice total, keep the invoice
open for the remaining balance and flag it for human review. Check whether
the difference is an agreed discount, a bank fee, a disputed charge, or a
partial payment. These are possible explanations, not confirmed causes.
Prepare a clarification request. Do not automatically write off the balance.

SECTION 2: Overpayments
When payments exceed the invoice total, flag the excess for human review.
Check for duplicate payments or payments intended for another invoice.
Do not automatically refund money or allocate it to another invoice.

SECTION 3: Exact payments
When the invoice reference matches and the received amount equals the
invoice total, mark the invoice as paid. Record the matching action.

SECTION 4: Review history
Record imported transaction identifiers, amounts, matching results, and
review decisions. A generated explanation is advice for a reviewer;
it must not independently change invoice amounts or approve refunds.
